# Code of conduct

Be respectful, discuss evidence and code rather than attacking people, and welcome
contributors with different backgrounds and experience. Harassment, threats, discriminatory
abuse and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.
Maintainers may edit/remove disruptive content or restrict participation. Raise routine
moderation concerns without reposting private information; private security concerns follow
SECURITY.md. No additional reporting address or response-time promise is implied.
